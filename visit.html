Your first visit

Four simple steps.

  1. Book

    By phone, WhatsApp or the form above. Tell us if you need a test, so it can be scheduled on the same visit.

  2. Bring

    Previous reports and scans, a list of your medicines, your glasses, and your inhaler if you use one.

  3. See the doctor

    A consultation with the specialist. If tests are needed, they’re done in the clinic’s lab.

  4. Results and follow-up

    Results come back to the same doctor, who goes through them with you and plans what happens next.

Visiting consultants.

Specialists from other departments see patients at AGR on their visiting days. Call the clinic for the current list and timings.
