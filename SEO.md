# SEO plan: AGR Medical Center, Yelahanka

**Goal:** appear at the top when people in North Bangalore search for a general physician, a
pulmonologist (chest specialist) or an eye specialist, especially in the Google Maps results
("near me" searches).

## A note on "best"

Writing "best" on the website won't make Google rank you for "best pulmonologist in North
Bangalore". For local searches Google weighs three things: **relevance** (does the page match the
search), **distance** (how close the clinic is to the searcher) and **prominence** (reviews,
ratings, links and mentions across the web). "Best" searches lean heavily on review count and
rating.

India's medical ethics rules for doctors also restrict self-promotional claims, so the site
describes what the doctors do instead of calling them the best. Let patients' reviews say it.

## Week 1: the steps that matter most

1. **Google Business Profile.** This is the single biggest factor for Maps and "near me" results.
   - Create or claim "AGR Medical Center" at business.google.com.
   - Primary category: *Medical clinic*. Add secondary categories: *Pulmonologist*,
     *Ophthalmologist*, *Eye care center*, *General practitioner*, *Medical laboratory*.
   - Use the exact address and phone from the website, and drop the map pin on the building
     itself.
   - Add opening hours (Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 9 pm), mention that walk-ins are welcome, a website link, and an appointment link (`/visit.html#book`).
   - Add real photos: the building with its signboard, reception, the lab, the eye examination
     room and both doctors. Add more every month.
   - List services (PFT, allergy tests, cataract surgery and so on) and write the description in
     plain words that name the specialties and Yelahanka.
   - Each doctor can also have their own practitioner profile at the same address, for example
     "Dr. Gangi Reddy TK – Pulmonologist".
2. **Domain and hosting.** Put the site on its own domain with HTTPS. Then replace `YOUR-DOMAIN` in
   `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`. Canonical tags and absolute social-image links can be added at
   the same time.
3. **Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.** Verify the domain in both and submit
   `sitemap.xml`.
4. **Reviews.** Ask every patient who had a good visit to leave a Google review.
   - Put a QR code at reception, and add the review link to the follow-up WhatsApp message.
   - Reply to every review, good or bad, without discussing medical details.
   - Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews; that breaks Google's rules.

## Weeks 2 to 4

5. **Directory listings.** List the clinic, and each doctor separately, on Practo, Justdial,
   Lybrate, Sulekha, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect (for Apple Maps). Link each listing to
   the website.
6. **Keep the name, address and phone identical everywhere,** character for character:
   AGR Medical Center, Lakshmi Ganapati Nagar, #25, off Kogilu Main Road, Yelahanka, Bengaluru,
   Karnataka 560064, +91 88613 56060.
7. **Show credentials.** Add each doctor's degrees, fellowships and medical registration number to
   their page (there's a marked spot). Google holds health websites to a higher standard and looks
   for evidence of real expertise.
8. **Opening hours** (Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 9 pm) are on the website. Use exactly the same hours
   on the Google profile and every listing.

## Every month

9. **One helpful article,** written or reviewed by one of the doctors and signed with their name.
   Answer questions people in the area actually search. For example:
   - When should a cough that won't go away be seen by a chest specialist?
   - Snoring and sleep apnoea: do you need a sleep study?
   - How often should people with diabetes have an eye check?
   - Cataract surgery: how do you know it's time?
   - Asthma or COPD: what's the difference?
10. **Local links and mentions.** Run health talks or screening camps (lung function, diabetic eye
    checks) with apartment associations, schools and offices in Yelahanka, Jakkur and Hebbal.
    Ask them to link to the site, and post on community apps where allowed.
11. **Google profile posts:** a short weekly health tip or update, and answers in the Q&A section.
12. **Measure:** Search Console (which searches show the site), Google Business Profile insights
    (calls, direction requests, website clicks), and the number of WhatsApp booking requests.
